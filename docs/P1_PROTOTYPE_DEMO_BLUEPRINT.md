## Investor five-minute script

### Minute 1 — Context

Show P0 Business Context.

Say:

> SEO OS knows what the business sells, who buys, what conversion matters, and which market matters.

### Minute 2 — First-party evidence

Open Command Center.

Say:

> P1 connects that business context to first-party search and analytics evidence.

### Minute 3 — Attention

Show Traffic Decline / CTR Opportunity / Striking Distance.

Say:

> The SEO lead does not have to scan every report. SEO OS surfaces meaningful changes.

### Minute 4 — Traceability

Open a Page Detail and Signal.

Say:

> Every signal is tied to the actual page, period, and source evidence.

### Minute 5 — Bridge to P3

Say:

> Notice we have not claimed why the decline happened. P3 will run a controlled evidence-based diagnosis before recommending action.
