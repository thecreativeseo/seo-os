# SEO OS — INVESTOR_DEMO_SCRIPT.md

## One-line product description

> SEO OS is a connected operating system for SEO teams that combines business context, first-party evidence, prioritization, AI-assisted diagnosis, controlled execution, and learning.

## Demo structure

### 1. P0 — Context

Open Business Context.

Say:

> Most SEO platforms start with a domain and immediately show charts. SEO OS first learns what the business sells, who the customer is, which conversion matters, the market, goals, competitors, and operating rules.

Show:

- Google login
- website-first onboarding
- Business Goals
- Context
- Brand Facts
- SEO Rules
- Connections

### 2. P1 — Evidence

Open Command Center.

Say:

> Once first-party systems are connected, SEO OS stops being setup software and becomes an operational intelligence layer.

Show:

- data freshness
- organic clicks
- impressions
- CTR
- organic sessions
- conversions
- Attention
- Winners / Losers

### 3. Trace to a page

Click Traffic Decline.

Show Page Detail.

Say:

> The system traces the observation to a specific page, queries, period, and source evidence.

### 4. Explain the product discipline

Open Signal.

Say:

> This is intentionally a signal, not a diagnosis. SEO OS will not invent a cause just because an LLM can produce a plausible explanation.

### 5. P4 M5 - the QA gate (about three minutes)

Open the demo website's Command Center.

Say:

> Everything so far decides what to write. This is what happens before anything is allowed out.

Point at the three QA tiles: **QA blockers**, **Awaiting final approval**, **Approved for CMS**.

#### The blocker

Click **QA blockers**, then open the blocked piece.

Show **Brand facts: Failed**, and the blocking finding on it.

Say:

> This piece was written and editorially approved while that fact was approved. The business has since withdrawn it. QA checks the facts as they stand today, not as they stood when the writer worked, so the piece is refused.

Point out that the finding is marked **Measured**.

Say:

> No model was asked. This is a lookup against the approved facts, and it is the kind of check that is allowed to block.

#### The report that passed

Go back and open **Awaiting final approval**, then the report.

Show the ten checks, and the **Measured** and **AI judged** labels beside them.

Say:

> Ten checks on one exact revision. The measured ones read the words and the rules. The judged ones answer the questions no lookup can: does this serve the search it was written for, does it answer what the brief asked, does it sound like this business.

Open one AI-judged finding.

Say:

> A judgment is a warning at most, and it carries the passage it is about, verified word for word against the approved revision. The model cannot mark anything blocking, and it cannot approve anything.

Scroll to **Not checked**.

Say:

> These were not checked at all, and they are not passes. The report says which, why, and whether that is missing evidence or a capability we have not configured.

#### The human gate

Scroll to **Approve for CMS**.

Show the revision number, the short hash, the QA run, the counts, and the acknowledgement.

Say:

> A person approves one exact revision, on one exact QA run, and accepts in writing the checks that could not run. That approval is a record, not a switch.

Approve it, and read the result out.

Say:

> Approved for CMS. Nothing has been published. No CMS has been touched. Execution is the next phase, and it is gated on this approval still being valid.

Point at **Publishing - Coming next** in the navigation.

Optionally show the stale case:

> If a fact or a rule moves after an approval, the approval stays on the record exactly as it was, and the product stops treating it as authorization. It says the QA behind it is stale, and asks for a fresh run and a fresh approval.

### 6. Show roadmap

```text
P0 Context
P1 Evidence
P2 Opportunity
P3 Diagnosis
P4 Execution
```

Say:

> P2 adds market, keyword and competitor opportunity intelligence. P3 diagnoses evidence with controlled AI workflows. P4 closes the loop with approved content and WordPress execution.

## Closing line

> The long-term moat is not another dashboard or prompt library. It is the retained relationship between business goals, demand, search evidence, decisions, changes, outcomes, and organizational learning.
