## Visual direction

Use a modern, credible B2B operations style:

- calm
- structured
- professional
- high information clarity
- restrained visual treatment
- strong hierarchy
- obvious next action

Avoid:

- fake AI glows
- fake analytics
- dashboard clutter
- lorem ipsum
- excessive gradients
