## Investor Five-Minute Script

### Minute 1
> P1 told us what changed.

Open a Page/Signal.

### Minute 2
> P2 combines that first-party evidence with keyword and competitor intelligence.

Show Top Opportunities.

### Minute 3
> This keyword should belong to the commercial page, but Google is currently ranking a supporting article.

Avoid saying cannibalization is confirmed.

### Minute 4
> Priority is transparent: business relevance, intent, demand, visibility, confidence and effort.

### Minute 5
> P2 tells us this is worth investigating. P3 performs the controlled diagnosis before recommending action.
